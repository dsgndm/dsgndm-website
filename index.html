02 — About

Brand, product and interface,
designed as one system.

I'm a Senior Product Designer and Design Lead with 20+ years in design, from branding and graphic design to UX/UI and product. Today I lead design at Kaavan, where I'm the sole designer of a B2B SaaS platform used by 300+ automotive dealership clients, and I own its design system end to end.

That range is my edge. I can take a product from first idea to a buildable interface — user flows, design systems, UI, brand identity and the front-end details developers need — without splitting the work across three people. I run client kickoffs, work side by side with developers, and turn feedback into clear requirements. I use AI tools to move faster; the design decisions stay mine.

My approach is user-centered and built on atomic design: every element has to make the product easier to use, more accessible and truer to the brand. I work fully in English (EOI C1) with international clients and remote teams.

Outside of work: videogames since the Amstrad CPC days, drawing on my Wacom, and ideas that take shape somewhere between trance music, sci-fi and strong coffee — usually with a cat on the desk.

"UI and UX go beyond aesthetics — they're about solving real problems, cleanly."
