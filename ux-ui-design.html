Services — UX/UI and Product Design

UX/UI and product design for SaaS and digital products

I design web and mobile products end to end — research, user flows, interfaces and developer handoff — for SaaS platforms, e-commerce and digital services. More than 20 years of experience across graphic design, branding and product, based in Santander (Spain), working with teams and clients across Europe and the United States.

What I do

I take a product from an unclear brief to interfaces that developers can build and users understand: user flows, wireframes, high-fidelity UI, prototypes and a design system that keeps everything consistent as the product grows.

The full product cycle

My work covers the full product design cycle: discovery and research, information architecture, user flows, wireframes, high-fidelity interfaces in Figma, interactive prototypes, usability testing and developer handoff. I design for desktop and mobile, with accessibility, real content and edge cases in mind from the first draft, not as a last-minute fix.

Design that carries a brand

I have spent more than 20 years in design, starting in graphic design and growing into UX/UI, product and brand. That is why the interfaces I design are not only usable but also coherent with a brand: typography, colour, imagery and tone are part of the product, not a layer added at the end.

International teams and clients

I work with international teams and clients. I was Lead Product & Brand Designer at Whizzle, an app built with a partner in Amsterdam for users in the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany and Switzerland, and I have designed for clients in Spain and the United States.

Owning the design side

I am used to being the only designer in the room. I lead the kickoff with the client, decide, present to stakeholders, document the work and support the build.

Why work with me

Most designers specialise in one layer. My profile connects several: graphic design, branding, UX/UI, product design and design systems. In practice, one person can define the brand, design the product and build the system that holds them together, with fewer handoffs, fewer inconsistencies and faster decisions.

With more than 20 years of experience and over 100 brands designed, I have worked with startups, agencies and SaaS companies in Spain, the Netherlands, the United States and across Europe. I am comfortable moving between strategy, craft and conversations with the client, and I use AI tools such as Magnific AI in my workflow for imagery and exploration, always with a design decision behind them.

What I enjoy most is taking an idea to a consistent, shippable design for a SaaS or e-commerce product, with the brand, the interface and the system working as one.

Who it's for

  • 01Startups and SaaS teams without in-house design that need someone to define the product and its interface from the first wireframe.
  • 02Product teams that need senior UX/UI direction: clear design decisions, made in close work with product managers and developers.
  • 03E-commerce brands that want to reduce cart abandonment, improve their product pages and raise conversion through clearer flows and better interfaces.
  • 04B2B and SaaS platforms with complex data, many roles or many customers, where the interface has to stay clear as features pile up.
  • 05Companies whose product has grown inconsistent and need to bring screens, components and patterns back into one system.
  • 06Agencies and studios that need a reliable senior UX/UI designer to join a project or cover peaks of work, in Spanish or English.

Questions

Which languages and time zone do you work in?

I'm based in Santander, Spain (CET) and work in Spanish or English.

Which tools do you use?

Figma for design and prototyping, Miro for workshops, and Jira or Notion to stay in sync with product and development teams.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope: a focused landing page or flow can take two to three weeks, while a full product redesign or design system usually takes a few months, often as an ongoing collaboration.

Can you work with our developers?

Yes. I design with technical constraints in mind, document components and stay available during implementation.

How do you work with other teams?

I have worked as the only designer of a product, as lead designer and alongside other designers, and I adapt to the size and the processes of each team.

Which sectors have you worked in?

Mostly SaaS and B2B platforms, e-commerce, consumer apps and digital marketing, with clients in Spain, the Netherlands and the United States.
