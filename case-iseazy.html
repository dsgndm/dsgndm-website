06 — UX/UI · SaaS Landing Page

iSEAZY@Work

iSEAZY, an e-learning company already trusted by names like Vodafone, BBVA and Orange, needed a landing page for a new product launch: iSEAZY@Work, language learning built for fifteen minutes a day at your desk.

ClientiSEAZY
RoleUX/UI Designer
TimelineFreelance project
ToolsFigma, Miro, Lottie

Overview

iSEAZY is an established e-learning platform trusted by companies like Vodafone, BBVA, Mapfre, Orange and Ted Baker. iSEAZY@Work is a new product within that family: language learning designed to fit inside a workday, in fifteen minutes a day.

My brief was to design a responsive landing page based on the existing brand guidelines that could carry a new product launch — from first impression through to a booked demo.

Challenge

  • 01The page had to launch a brand-new product name while staying unmistakably part of the trusted iSEAZY family.
  • 02Three distinct pricing tiers — Professional, Business and Enterprise — needed to be easy to compare at a glance.
  • 03The core pitch, fifteen minutes of learning a day, had to come through as the headline benefit, not buried in feature lists.
  • 04A multi-field demo-request form needed to feel quick to fill in despite asking for real qualifying information.

Process

I designed the page as one journey from first impression to a booked demo: a hero built on the fifteen-minutes-a-day promise, a feature grid, a native-app section, three pricing tiers with a monthly and yearly toggle, social proof and the demo form — with matching mobile layouts.

Solution

A responsive landing page — built on iSEAZY's brand system — covering the product pitch, tiered pricing, social proof from existing enterprise clients, and a demo-request flow shaped by real user research.
