Services — Branding and Identity

Brand identity and branding design

From the first logo sketch to a complete brand book: I build identities that are clear, consistent and ready to use across product, web, social media and print — for startups and growing companies, in Spain and abroad, that need their brand to look as serious as their product. More than 100 brands designed in over 20 years.

FocusBrand identity, logos, brand books
Based inSantander, Spain · Remote
LanguagesSpanish, English
ToolsFigma, Miro, Notion, Jira

What I do

I define how a brand looks, sounds and behaves, and then turn it into assets people can actually use: logo suite, colour and typography, imagery, guidelines and the templates that keep every new piece on-brand.

Identity, voice and assets

My branding work covers logo and symbol design, colour, typography, imagery, tone of voice, brand books and the templates for social media, newsletters, presentations, packaging and web. I start with the business, the audience and the competition, because a good identity is a decision about positioning before it is a drawing.

100+ brands in Spain, Europe and the US

In more than 20 years I have designed over 100 brands for clients in Spain, the Netherlands and the United States: an app built with a partner in Amsterdam (Whizzle), a premium skincare brand based in California (Ayucell), a geoanalytics platform (MapLab.world), a marketplace for vintage tech (Robotyard) and many more, from startups to established companies.

Brand and product together

Because I also design interfaces and design systems, I think about how a brand behaves in real use — buttons, forms, social posts, emails, packaging — and not only how a logo looks on a mockup. Guidelines are written for the people who will use them: marketing teams, developers, freelancers and agencies.

AI-assisted imagery, with criteria

I also use AI-assisted imagery as part of the brand toolkit, always with clear criteria: for Ayucell I created image guidelines so the client can keep producing consistent visuals in the future.

Why work with me

Brand design with product thinking. I have designed brands together with the apps, websites and design systems that carry them, so identities I create work on screen, in real interfaces and in marketing from day one — not only on a presentation board.

My profile is multidisciplinary: graphic design, branding, UX/UI, product design and design systems. After more than 20 years, I can take a brand from strategy and positioning to logo, brand book, website and launch campaign, working remotely with clients in Spain, Europe and the United States in Spanish or English.

Questions

Do you also design the website or app?

Yes. I have built brands together with the sites and products that carry them, so the identity works on screen from day one.

What do I receive at the end?

The final logo files, the brand book and the source files, so your team or other designers can keep working with them.

How do we start?

With a short conversation about your business, audience and competitors, before any design work begins.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. I have worked with clients in Spain, the Netherlands and the United States, remotely and in English or Spanish, adapting tone and visuals to each market.

Can you work with an existing logo?

Yes. When a brand already has a logo it wants to keep, I build the guidelines, colour, typography, tone and imagery around it, and I suggest improvements only where they add value.
