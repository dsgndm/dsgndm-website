08 — Social media · Newsletter

China Tours

China Tours is a German-language travel brand specialising in trips to China. After its brand refresh, its newsletter and social media needed to feel as inviting and trustworthy as the destination itself.

ClientChina Tours
RoleSocial Media & Newsletter Designer
ScopeNewsletter, social media
ToolsFigma, Affinity Designer, Miro

Overview

China Tours, with the tagline “Dein Weg ins Reich der Mitte”, sells small-group tours of China to a German-speaking audience. I designed its social media and newsletter in Figma and Affinity Designer as part of the brand refresh.

Challenge

  • 01Build trust quickly with first-time visitors: clear promises, awards and ratings had to be visible without cluttering the message.
  • 02Let the destination lead: landscapes and people, not stock-travel clichés.
  • 03Design a newsletter that reads well on a phone, with one clear call to action.
  • 04Keep the social feed instantly recognisable as part of the refreshed brand.

Solution

A mobile-first newsletter template that opens with a landscape hero, states four brand promises, adds social proof (an industry award and a 4.7/5 rating) and closes on a single call to action, plus a photography-led social feed concept built on the same visual language.
