Services — Design Systems

Design systems for scalable digital products

I build and scale design systems in Figma: colour, type and spacing foundations, variable-based components, documentation and the habits that make teams actually use them. I built one from scratch for a consumer app.

FocusDesign systems, component libraries
Based inSantander, Spain
LanguagesSpanish, English
ToolsFigma, Miro, Notion, Jira

What I do

A design system is the shared source that lets a product grow without falling apart. I audit what exists, define the foundations and build the components, with variants and auto layout that survive real, variable content.

Multi-brand by design

A system can also be multi-brand: with Figma variables and modes, the same components adapt to very different brand styles without breaking.

Whizzle: built from scratch

At Whizzle I built a complete design system from scratch for a consumer app, with an 8-point base, light and dark versions, and a brand book, so product and brand shared one visual language from the start.

Structure and way of working

I organise Figma libraries in separate, independent files, with their own libraries for logos, icons and typefaces, and I recommend a mobile-first approach, so the system stays fast to use and easy for developers to translate into code.

Why work with me

I have been on the side that builds and on the side that uses a design system. I know what makes teams adopt one — clear naming, components that survive real content, fast documentation — and what makes them ignore it. That is why the systems I build are practical first and elegant second.

My profile is multidisciplinary: graphic design, branding, UX/UI, product design and design systems. After more than 20 years, I can connect the brand, the interface and the system in one coherent piece of work, collaborating with teams in Spain, Europe and the United States.

I like owning the system and the product around it, so design and code stay in step as the product grows.

Questions

When does a company need a design system?

When several people design the product, screens start to look different, or development repeats interface work and each new feature takes longer than the last.

Do you build the coded component library?

I design and document the system in Figma and work closely with front-end developers on tokens, naming and specs; the code library itself is built with your development team.

Can you improve an existing system instead of starting again?

Yes. That is often the better route: audit what works, restructure what doesn't and migrate step by step without stopping the product.

Can a design system support several brands or clients?

Yes. With Figma variables and modes, the same components can adapt to different brand styles.

Do you work with international teams?

Yes. I work with teams and clients in Spain, the Netherlands and across Europe, in Spanish or English, and I am used to asynchronous work and clear documentation.
